About

Rawkaki

At rawkaki we believe that ordinary things are rarely ordinary when you take the time to really look at them.

Rawkaki is a photography and print-on-demand art project built around curiosity, observation, and a slightly different way of seeing everyday life. From quiet corners of nature and unexpected details to familiar scenes with a playful twist, our work is about finding something worth noticing in the things that usually pass us by.

Behind rawkaki is a photographer shaped by years of working across commercial photography, photojournalism, food, portraits, architecture, travel, and social media content. From photographing current events in the streets of Athens to chasing landscapes across 15 countries in Europe, the camera has always been a way of exploring, understanding, and expressing the world.

Some rawkaki pieces are inspired by nature. Others come from everyday objects, places, moods, or little moments that somehow deserve a second look. The common thread is simple: a familiar subject, seen from an unfamiliar angle.

Rawkaki is about curiosity, creativity, and finding the extraordinary hiding in plain sight.

Look twice. You might see something new.
